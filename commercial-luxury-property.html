Luxury property development

A bilingual English and French website for a luxury residential development, with image-led storytelling and private viewing enquiries.

Sector
Luxury property development
My role
Developed from supplied designs
Built with
WordPress, ACF, WPML, Gravity Forms, Cookiebot, SCSS and Gulp
Scale
25 blocks, in English and French
AT A GLANCE
  • Languages: English and French through WPML, with a fixed-order language switcher
  • Media: Video and image heroes with separate mobile versions and a lightbox
  • Maps: Image-based site and area maps with toggles and mobile versions
  • Forms: A private viewing enquiry in Gravity Forms

1 · What it needed

What the site had to do.

A luxury development sells on atmosphere and detail, to an audience that includes French speakers. The site needed to be image-led and slow-paced, work properly in both languages, and make asking for a private viewing easy.

2 · What a visitor can do

The site in use.

  • Land on a full-width image or video, with a switch between EN and FR in the header.
  • Scroll through lifestyle and heritage sections where images and video fade and move into place.
  • Open images in a lightbox with captions, and switch between plans or maps with toggles.
  • Jump between sections from quick links, then request a private viewing.

3 · What I built

What I built, and how.

English and French

The site runs on WPML with ACF and Gravity Forms translation support. I wrote a compact language switcher that outputs English and French in a fixed order, shows the current language as plain text and the other as a link, and returns nothing if WPML is not active.

Media that suits the device

Hero and content media wrappers accept a video, an image and separate mobile versions of each. Videos autoplay muted and inline, with an option to add any item to a captioned lightbox gallery. An inline SVG helper lets editors drop in line art that can still be styled.

Image-led layouts

Most of the build is a set of blocks designed for large photography: large-media sections, captioned and inline media wraps, half and wide image-and-text variants and a timeline whose entries can use an image or a video. A map block lets visitors switch between several maps with toggle buttons, each with an optional mobile version.

Viewing enquiries

The enquiry block pairs editable introduction text with a Gravity Form, and the introduction is set up to disappear once the form is submitted so the confirmation stands alone. The legal content has its own block, and in-page navigation targets let quick links jump to a section.

4 · Why this approach

Why I built it this way.

A purpose-built language switcher. A function that asks WPML for the available languages and returns English and French in a fixed order. The default WPML switcher offers more than this two-language site needs.
