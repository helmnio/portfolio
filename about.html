Client work

Since 2019 I have been a web developer at Gooey, a white-label agency, so the sites I build carry other agencies' brands. My job is to take a supplied design and brief and turn it into a site that works and that the client can run themselves.

My work starts before the build. I join client calls, listen to what the business needs, and talk them through how it can be done with WordPress and the right plugins, or with a custom plugin when nothing off the shelf fits. From there I plan the approach with designers and account teams, build the custom theme, functionality and integrations, and set up the editing tools people will use afterwards. I stay involved through launch and support.

What I enjoy most is problem solving: taking a complex or vague need, breaking it into smaller parts, and finding a solution that is simple to use and straightforward to maintain.

The result I aim for is the same each time: a client's designs and needs transformed into a clean, well-structured product that is easy to use and easy to keep up to date.

Two examples show the range:

Property data and availability

A new-homes developer needed buyers to find developments, house types and individual plots, and register interest in the right one. The plot data comes from the client's own system through a separate integration. My side was the theme: working out each development's lowest price and remaining plots, tidying inconsistent plot numbers and availability labels, and making interest forms that know which plot or development the visitor is looking at.

Finding the nearest stockist or dealer

On a trade catalogue and a vehicle dealer network, visitors needed to find who is closest, and the businesses needed to keep their own details current. I built the location search and the update routes, with each submission held for review until an administrator approves it.

How I approach a build

Build for the person who edits the site

A site is only useful if the people running it can keep it up to date. I give editors flexible page blocks to assemble new pages themselves, sensible user roles, and review steps for content submitted by others, so submitted changes can be checked before they are published.

Make the right thing easy to find

Whether it is a property, a stockist or a holiday itinerary, visitors need to narrow things down quickly. I build search, filters and maps around how people look for each thing.

Connect the site to the business

Enquiry forms, calculators and data feeds should end up with the right person in a usable form, so I work out where each piece of information needs to go before building the form.

Using AI on a client build

On a new-homes site, plot numbers and availability labels arrive from the source system in different forms. I used Claude to list the formats the data could take and to draft tests for the helper functions, then checked every case against real plot data myself before it shipped.
