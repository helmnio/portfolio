1 · What it needed

What the site had to do.

A consultancy sells on credibility. The site needed to explain what the firm does clearly, introduce the people behind it and make contact simple, while being easy for the team to keep up to date.

2 · What a visitor can do

The site in use.

  • Read what the firm does in a services block that pairs an image with a list of services.
  • Meet the team in a slider that shows each person’s photo and role.
  • Read insights and download documents.
  • Use in-page links to move around long pages, then get in touch.

3 · What I built

What I built, and how.

A small, focused block set

Services, team, insights, downloads, FAQs, quotes, a map and a call to action, plus in-page navigation targets so long pages can be linked into. Heroes come in two variants, one image-led with a content panel and link.

Content the team can update

The team block pulls selected profile posts into a slider with each person’s role. The insights block shows either hand-picked articles in the order chosen or the latest, and works with FacetWP. Downloads are a simple list of titled files. Menu items can carry a line of extra text, set in the menu editor.

4 · Why this approach

Why I built it this way.

Keep it small. A short block library built for this one site’s content. A reader of a consultancy site wants clear answers, not lots of components.
