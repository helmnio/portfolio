About me

Profile

WordPress and WooCommerce developer with 8+ years of commercial experience, the last seven at Gooey Creative, a white-label agency. I build custom themes, plugins and integrations for client sites in property, travel, automotive and eCommerce, working from supplied designs and specifications. I like owning a build from technical planning through launch and support, and I build for the people who will edit the site: reusable ACF blocks, sensible roles and clear editor warnings. Outside client work I build and publish my own products, including the ArchticFrame WordPress plugin and the Helmn strategy game.

Technical skills

  • WordPress: custom themes and plugins, ACF and Gutenberg block libraries (19 to 48 layouts per site), custom post types and taxonomies, REST APIs, editor roles and workflows
  • Search and data: FacetWP, Property Hive, custom queries, proximity search, Google Maps, external data feeds and scheduled sync
  • Commerce and forms: WooCommerce (product configuration, subscriptions, filtering), Gravity Forms and payment workflows, CRM integrations
  • Front end: JavaScript ES6+, React, jQuery, HTML, SCSS, Bootstrap, responsive design, accessibility, WPML for bilingual sites
  • Workflow: PHP, Git, Composer, npm, Gulp, Linux, performance tuning and Core Web Vitals, production support
