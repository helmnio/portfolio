About me

Profile

Web developer with 8+ years of commercial experience, specialising in bespoke WordPress, WooCommerce and PHP, the last seven at Gooey Creative, a white-label agency. I build the practical parts that make a site easy to run: custom themes and plugins, reusable editing components, approval workflows and API integrations, and I take work from planning through launch and support. I'm also comfortable investigating and maintaining sites built by other teams. AI is part of my daily workflow, always with human review. Outside client work I build my own projects to learn new stacks and try ideas, from React and TypeScript to Node and MCP.
